Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Beyond The Visa currently uses necessary browser storage and similar device technologies to provide sign-in, security, preferences and features you request. It does not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site behavioural tracking.

What is stored

  • Authentication and security state.
  • Country, profession and interface preferences.
  • Journey, learning and saved-feature state.
  • Your cookie-notice acknowledgement and storage choice.

Why necessary storage is used

This storage makes the service work, protects signed-in sessions and remembers actions requested by the user. Disabling or clearing it may sign the user out and remove locally stored preferences or progress.

Optional technologies

Analytics, advertising and cross-site tracking storage are not currently enabled. If that changes, this notice and the in-app preference centre must be updated and prior consent requested where required. Rejecting optional technologies will be presented as clearly as accepting them.

Managing storage

Users can review current choices in Privacy & Legal → Your data & choices. Browser settings can also clear site information. Clearing browser storage does not necessarily delete cloud account records or uploaded files.

External websites

Regulators, employers and other websites opened from Beyond The Visa control their own cookies and privacy practices.

Contact

Use Contact Us and choose “Privacy or data request” for questions about storage technology or privacy rights.
